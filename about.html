Bio

Abdullah Al Mazid

Abdullah Al Mazid is a final-year Industrial & Production Engineering student at the Bangladesh University of Engineering & Technology (BUET), graduating in 2026 with a CGPA of 3.67/4.00. His work spans operations research, quality engineering, and manufacturing systems, with a growing focus on applying machine learning and computer vision to industrial problems — most visibly through his undergraduate thesis, AutoPrompt-SAM, an automatic defect-segmentation pipeline for CFRP thermography. He has co-authored three peer-reviewed conference papers across IEEE and IEOM, and completed an industrial attachment at PRAN-RFL Group studying large-scale FMCG production. He is currently open to opportunities in manufacturing, operations, quality, and data-driven industrial roles.
