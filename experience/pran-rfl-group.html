Industrial Attachment

PRAN-RFL Group

A month-long industrial attachment observing large-scale FMCG production and the operational systems that keep it running.

Dhaka, Bangladesh 05 Nov – 05 Dec 2025

Overview

During this attachment, exposure spanned beverage, dairy, biscuit, and confectionery production lines within the PRAN-RFL Group, covering end-to-end production and supply chain operations at FMCG scale.

What the attachment covered

  • Observed large-scale FMCG production across beverage, dairy, biscuit, and confectionery lines, gaining exposure to end-to-end production and supply chain operations.
  • Studied time-and-motion analysis, production scheduling, and statistical quality control practices in a live manufacturing environment.
  • Assessed industrial automation systems, operational control mechanisms, and inventory management practices at scale.
