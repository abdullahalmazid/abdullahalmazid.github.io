SolidWorks · Advanced 3D CAD Design

Shantui Compact Bulldozer

A highly detailed 3D CAD model of a Shantui compact bulldozer, replicating real-world engineering structure and functionality.

SolidWorks Ansys

Overview

As part of SolidWorks training, independently designed a highly detailed 3D model of a Shantui compact bulldozer, replicating real-world engineering structure and functionality. The project involved precise modeling of critical components — including the blade mechanism, track system, chassis, and operator cabin — followed by a complete assembly reflecting realistic machine architecture. Advanced CAD techniques were applied throughout, focusing on dimensional accuracy, mechanical alignment, and structural integrity.

Skills applied

  • 3D modeling and assembly design in SolidWorks
  • Mechanical visualization of complex industrial machinery
  • Construction-equipment design principles and engineering realism
