Validation of Temperature Effect on 3D Printed PLA Materials using FEA & Hyperelastic Modeling

Venue: 7th IEOM Bangladesh 2024 Type: Conference Paper

Abstract

Published under the full title "Validation of Temperature Effect on 3D Printed PLA Materials with Finite Element Analysis and Nonlinear Hyperelastic Modeling." Additive manufacturing (3D printing) has simplified how non-metallic materials such as hyperelastic and thermoplastic substances are designed and tested, but accurately modeling and validating their behavior remains difficult. This study numerically validates the mechanical behavior of 3D-printed polylactic acid (PLA) under three temperature conditions (20°C, 30°C, and 40°C), building on prior experimental work. Dog-bone specimens were designed to the ASTM D638 standard with 100% infill density and simulated in Ansys Mechanical using three nonlinear hyperelastic models: Neo-Hookean, two-parameter Mooney-Rivlin, and third-order Yeoh. All three models fit the experimental stress-strain and strain-energy data well, with the Yeoh model matching most closely at higher temperatures and the single-parameter Neo-Hookean model offering the simplest fit. The study also derives a new equation estimating the Neo-Hookean model's material parameter as a function of temperature, offering a simplified way to model PLA's behavior across different thermal conditions.

Authors

Md Zisanul Haque Bhuiyan, Asif Hasan, Abdullah Al Mazid, Ehasanul Islam Rafi, and Shadman Shihab — Department of Industrial & Production Engineering, BUET.
