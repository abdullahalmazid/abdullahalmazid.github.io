eGov-Lens: Multi-Dimensional ML for Aspect-Based Bengali Public Feedback Analysis

Venue: 28th ICCIT 2025 (IEEE) Type: Conference Paper

Abstract

Published under the full title "eGov-Lens: A Multi-Dimensional Machine Learning Approach to Aspect-Based Public Feedback Analysis on Bengali e-Government Platforms." As government services in Bangladesh have moved online, citizens increasingly voice feedback about them on social media, but existing sentiment tools miss the specific, service-level detail policymakers need. This paper introduces eGov-Lens, an aspect-based feedback pipeline built on a newly curated set of over 34,000 annotated Bengali comments spanning twelve government services, each labeled across four dimensions: overall sentiment, ease of finding a service, responsiveness, and accuracy. Three text representations (a bag-of-words baseline, BanglaBERT, and mBERT) were benchmarked against logistic regression, SVC, and naïve Bayes classifiers. A BanglaBERT model with an SVC head reached the strongest accuracy on the policy-critical accuracy aspect (≈93%), while BanglaBERT paired with logistic regression offered the best balance of accuracy and speed for real deployment, and a lightweight bag-of-words baseline trained and ran fastest. An entropy-weighted TOPSIS ranking combined quality and computational cost across all candidate models, favoring the lightweight baseline overall for resource-constrained government infrastructure, with the BanglaBERT + logistic regression combination a close second.

Authors

Md. Muqtadir Fuad, Abdullah Al Mazid, Md. Istiak Adnan, Tirthojit Das, Shatabdi Sen Prapti, and Nafisa Mahbub — Department of Industrial & Production Engineering, BUET.
