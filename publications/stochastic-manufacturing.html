Stochastic Modeling of Throughput-Quality Dynamics in Labor-Intensive Manufacturing

Venue: 8th IEOM Bangladesh 2025 Type: Conference Paper

Abstract

Full title: "Stochastic Modeling of Throughput-Quality Dynamics in Labor-Intensive Manufacturing: A Comparative Analysis of Operator Fatigue and Process Instability." Manual assembly lines in semi-automated food production face persistent standardization and quality-assurance challenges due to natural variability in human performance. This study quantifies and compares how operator fatigue behaves stochastically across two manual production lines, combining high-resolution time-and-motion analysis with statistical process control to relate product shape, operator behavior, efficiency, and unit-weight consistency. Regression analysis characterized how worker fatigue develops over time under controlled conditions, and process capability analysis linked that fatigue to process instability. A Monte Carlo simulation was then used to estimate the resulting annual financial loss from process instability, translating the statistical findings into a concrete business impact — an estimated 1.7 million BDT per year across the two product lines studied.

Authors

Md. Muqtadir Fuad, Abdullah Al Mazid, Md. Istiak Adnan, and Khalid Ammar — Department of Industrial & Production Engineering, BUET.
